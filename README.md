## Why Spendii

Most people have no clear picture of where their money goes. Existing tools often feel like chores, ask for too much personal data, or bury the useful parts behind complexity.

Spendii is built around a simple idea: **tracking your money should take seconds, not effort, and it should stay yours.**

## Our intentions

- **Effortless.** Capturing a spend should feel as quick as taking a screenshot.
- **Private by design.** Your financial life is personal. We aim to collect as little as possible and keep control with you.
- **Clear.** Turn raw activity into insights that help you make better decisions, without the jargon.
- **Calm and pleasant.** A finance app people actually enjoy opening, with a clean look and room to make it your own.
- **For everyone.** Useful for individuals, and for the people they share expenses with.

## What the app helps you do

At a high level, Spendii is designed to help you:

- Record spending quickly and keep it organized
- Understand patterns in where your money goes
- Plan ahead with budgets and savings targets
- Stay on top of recurring costs and shared money
- Keep your data safe, portable and under your control

The website presents the product, its approach to privacy, and where to get help. Detailed product information is available on the [live site](https://sambit650.github.io/web.spendii/).

## About this repository

This repo contains the **marketing and information website** only. It includes the landing page and the About, Privacy, Terms and Support pages. It does not contain the app itself.

### Built with

Next.js, React, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS, deployed as a static site on GitHub Pages through GitHub Actions.

### Run it locally

Requires Node.js 20 or newer.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/Sambit650/web.spendii.git
cd web.spendii
npm install
npm run dev
```

Then open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000).

### Deployment

Pushing to `main` builds and publishes the site automatically through the workflow in `.github/workflows`. In the repository, set **Settings → Pages → Source** to **GitHub Actions** once.
